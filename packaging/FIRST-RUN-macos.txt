CIU for macOS (Apple Silicon)
=============================

Drag CIU.app to your Applications folder.

This build is not signed with an Apple Developer certificate, so macOS will
refuse to open it until you say otherwise. That is Gatekeeper doing its job on
software it cannot trace to a registered developer, and it happens with any
unsigned app, not just this one.

One command clears it.


FIRST RUN
---------

1. Drag CIU.app to Applications.

2. Open Terminal and paste this, then press Return:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/CIU.app

3. Open CIU from Applications, Launchpad or Spotlight.

A Terminal window opens showing what CIU is doing, and your browser opens at
the page. Leave that Terminal window open; closing it stops CIU.


IF STEP 2 REPORTS "No such xattr"
---------------------------------

Nothing is wrong. The app was never quarantined, usually because it was moved
with the command line rather than downloaded in a browser. Carry on to step 3.


THE OTHER WAY, WITHOUT TERMINAL
-------------------------------

Double-click CIU.app. macOS blocks it. Open System Settings, go to Privacy and
Security, scroll to the bottom, and there will be a line saying CIU was
blocked, with an "Open Anyway" button. Click it, then open CIU again and
confirm.


WHY A TERMINAL WINDOW
---------------------

CIU runs a small server on your machine. The window is both the log, which is
the first place to look when something goes wrong, and the off switch.


WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
-----------------

CIU does not include the inference engine. The page offers to fetch the Metal
build of llama-server, about 20 MB, and then offers to download a model, which
is several gigabytes.

Both are one click and both show progress.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------

The Terminal window is the log. Whatever it says is the real error, and it is
more useful than anything the page shows.
